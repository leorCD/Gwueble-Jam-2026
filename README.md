# GO FISH

You're a fish who has to make money for his family the only way he can: gambling. The more you lose, the darker the game gets, but your wins get bigger too.

<img width="615" height="150" alt="ezgif com-resize" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1157814e-cbbb-41b4-9034-3dfd45a75809" />


## About
Made for Gwueble Jam 2026, a short game jam. It's a game about risk and desperation: as your losses pile up, the tone shifts from lighthearted to dark, and the stakes (and payouts) rise with it.

## Built With
- Godot 4 (C#)
- Blender (3D models)
